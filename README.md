# Subscription Analytics Project — Executive Summary

## 1. Customer Cohort Analysis
**Business Question:** After how many months do customers typically cancel their subscriptions?

**Answer:**  
Customers typically cancel their subscriptions after **3–4 months**.  
The cohort matrix shows a consistent drop‑off beginning at Month 3, with retention falling significantly by Month 4 across all cohorts.

---

## 2. Revenue Forecast
**Business Question:** What is the expected subscription revenue over the next 12 months?
Does the revenue forecast show evidence of seasonality?

**Answer:**  
The expected subscription revenue over the next 12 months shows a **steady upward trend**.  
The forecast does **not** show evidence of seasonality — the projected revenue curve 
increases smoothly without recurring monthly or quarterly patterns.

---

## 3. CAC vs. LTV Analysis
**Business Question:** How long does it take for the 2024 and 2025 cohorts to recover 
their customer acquisition cost?

**Answer:**  
Neither the 2024 nor the 2025 cohort reaches CAC break‑even.  
The cumulative LTV for both cohorts remains far below the Total CAC line, indicating 
that the revenue generated by each cohort is insufficient to recover acquisition costs.

---

## Summary
Overall, the analysis reveals:
- Customers churn early (Month 3–4)
- Revenue is expected to grow steadily with no seasonal fluctuations
- Acquisition costs are too high relative to customer lifetime value, preventing
- break-even

These insights suggest the company should focus on:
- Improving early retention  
- Reducing CAC  
- Increasing customer lifetime value through engagement or upsell strategies
