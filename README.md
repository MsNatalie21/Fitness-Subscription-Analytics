1. Customer Cohort Analysis
Business Question:  
After how many months do customers typically cancel their subscriptions?

Answer:  
Customers typically cancel their subscriptions after 3–4 months.
The cohort matrix shows a consistent drop‑off beginning at Month 3, with retention falling significantly by Month 4 across all cohorts.

The expected subscription revenue over the next 12 months shows a steady upward trend.

3. CAC vs. LTV Analysis
Business Question:  
How long does it take for the 2024 and 2025 cohorts to recover their customer acquisition cost?

Answer:  
Both cohorts reach CAC break‑even, but at different times:

2024 Cohort: Break‑even occurs in Month 3 (March).

2025 Cohort: Break‑even occurs earlier, in Month 2 (February).

This indicates that customers acquired in 2025 generate revenue more quickly than those acquired in 2024, suggesting improved acquisition efficiency or stronger early‑stage engagement.

Summary of Insights
Customers churn early, typically around Month 3–4.

Subscription revenue is expected to grow steadily over the next year, with no seasonal fluctuations detected.

The 2025 cohort reaches CAC break‑even one month earlier than the 2024 cohort.

After break‑even, both cohorts generate positive lifetime value, with 2025 showing stronger early performance.

Recommendations
Strengthen early‑stage retention to reduce churn in Months 3–4.

Continue optimizing acquisition strategies that improved 2025’s early revenue performance.

Explore upsell or engagement strategies to increase LTV beyond the break‑even point.
